

\# PriceRush — Data Sources



PriceRush uses crowdsourced product information and

observed retail prices.



\## Open Prices



Source: https://prices.openfoodfacts.org/



License: Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0



Open Prices provides observed retail prices, store

information, and observation dates.



These prices are historical observations and are

not guaranteed to represent current retail prices.



\## Open Food Facts



Source: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/



Product database license: ODbL 1.0



Product images: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike

(CC BY-SA). See the source's terms for applicable

version and attribution requirements.



Product information and images are contributed by

the Open Food Facts community.



\## Attribution



Price data: Open Prices contributors.

Product information and images: Open Food Facts contributors.



\## Reuse compliance



Before public deployment, PriceRush must:



\- Display source attribution and license links.

\- Provide appropriate attribution for product images.

\- Determine and satisfy ODbL share-alike obligations

&#x20; for the derived product database.

\- Review API usage terms and rate limits.

\- Preserve original source identifiers and dates.



\## Price accuracy



Prices shown in PriceRush represent recorded observations

at particular stores and dates.



They are not live price quotes.
